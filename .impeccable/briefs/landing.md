# Surface brief: Pathway (landing = Persuade; matches, detail, profile, tracker = Operate)

## Direction contract
THESIS: Pathway shows its work. The page is built like international signage: one strict grid, big tabular numerals, and colour used only where it carries meaning. It rejects the category default of a centred headline over a row of identical feature cards with a blue accent.

OWN-WORLD:
- Paper-white ground (#fbfbf9) and ink-black type in Archivo, heavy at display sizes.
- One flat ultramarine field (#1f3fd1) owns the hero and every major statement band.
- Status colours carry fixed meanings: yellow #ffd23f = verify, red #d6322b = blocked, green #1d8a4e = met.
- Square corners, rules instead of cards, and roles packed edge to edge, each with its reference code (CA-01).

STORY:
- The visitor understands that Pathway ranks international roles with visible arithmetic.
- They believe that fit and eligibility are kept apart.
- They load the sample profile or build their own.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- An ultramarine field spans 7 of 12 columns, holding a 4–5rem headline, a one-line promise and two working actions: "Try with a sample profile", which saves the profile and opens the matches, and "Build your profile".
- The right 5 columns hold a live score strip for a real role, computed by the engine, with six weighted segments, pinned notes and a separate eligibility tag.
- On phones the two stack, with the field first.

- From the toy catalogue: the ranked field is packed with a code ruled beneath each role.

- From the daylight section: notes pinned at exact positions with tabular figures.
